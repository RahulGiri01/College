Available Courses

BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)

Duration: 4 years

BCA is an undergraduate program that focuses on computer science and application development. It is ideal for students who aspire to enter the IT industry. The curriculum covers programming languages (C, C++, Java, Python), database management, networking, data structures, algorithms, and web development.

Career Opportunities: Software Developer, Web Developer, System Analyst, Data Analyst, IT Support Executive

BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

Duration: 4 years

BBA is a foundation course in business and management, aimed at students interested in corporate careers or entrepreneurship. It blends business theory with practical learning. Subjects include marketing, finance, human resource management, business law, economics, and business analytics.

Career Opportunities: Marketing Executive, Financial Analyst, HR Executive, Operations Manager, Business Development Associate

BBA (HM) - Hospitality Management

Duration: 4 years

This is a specialized BBA program that trains students in hospitality operations, customer service, tourism, and hotel management. The course blends theoretical knowledge with practical exposure to the hospitality industry.

Career Opportunities: Hotel Manager, Front Office Executive, Food & Beverage Manager, Event Coordinator, Travel Consultant

BOPTM (Bachelor of Optometry)

Duration: 4 years

BOPTM is a healthcare program focused on vision science and eye care. Students learn to diagnose, treat, and manage various eye-related issues. The course includes theory, practical sessions, and clinical training.

Career Opportunities: Optometrist, Vision Therapist, Optical Store Manager, Eye Care Consultant, Research Assistant in vision science
